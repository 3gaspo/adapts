HISTORICAL TS-IFA FINDINGS BEFORE THE CONFIGURABLE-ROUTING RESET
- All completed pilots used Electricity, Chronos-2, K=3, seed 1, and the coupled
  settings 168:24, 336:48, and 504:168; they do not isolate lookback from horizon.
- The obsolete combined-rooter implementation generalized poorly: mean held-out
  T3 nMSE improvement was -27.42% for its neural router and -14.58% for ridge.
- The isolated 10K joint-ridge raw pilot was the least-bad aggregate result at
  +0.04% mean T3 nMSE improvement: +3.26%, +0.58%, and -3.72% by setting.
- Extending joint ridge to 30K improved the selected T2 criterion but damaged
  T3: raw retrieval averaged +3.03% on T2 and -6.71% on T3; instance retrieval
  averaged +3.25% on T2 and -17.97% on T3.
- At 30K, raw/instance T3 nMSE improvements were +3.64%/+3.72% at 168:24,
  -0.50%/-15.02% at 336:48, and -23.29%/-42.60% at 504:168.
- Every 30K joint-ridge configuration worsened raw MSE and MAE. At 504:168,
  raw MSE was 175.54% worse than vanilla with raw retrieval and 369.76% worse
  with instance retrieval.
- Residual and memory were the unstable learned branches. At 504:168 their T3
  nMSE was 275%--745% worse than vanilla, while cov was only 11%--13% worse.
- At the selected 336:48 and 504:168 T2 checkpoints, every non-vanilla branch
  was individually worse than vanilla even though their signed combination was
  better, indicating fragile cancellation that did not transfer to T3.
- The duplicate 30K joint-neural jobs selected early checkpoints for four of
  six configurations and produced zero T3 wins; they were not independent runs.
- The strongest deployable screen method under matching retrieval beat every
  30K joint-ridge result and eight of nine isolated 10K configurations.
- These observations motivated the new 20K-only configurable routing contract:
  branch subsets, shared or horizon routing, and optional softmax constraints.

OVERALL TS-IFA ASSESSMENT
All synchronized TS-IFA evidence is limited to Electricity and seed 1. The
historical combined-rooter run is obsolete; among current isolated variants,
the raw joint-ridge 10K pilot remains the least-bad aggregate result at +0.04%
mean T3 nMSE improvement, but its raw MSE and MAE worsen and it loses badly at
504:168. The strongest deployable screen method under the same retrieval beats
every 30K joint-ridge configuration and eight of the nine current 10K isolated
configurations; raw joint-ridge 10K at 336:48 is the sole exception. TS-IFA
therefore remains an architecture-tuning track and should not enter the
publication benchmark. The next useful pilot must constrain or freeze the
unstable residual/memory branches and select against scale-sensitive failure;
additional epochs or an instance-retrieval expansion are not justified.
