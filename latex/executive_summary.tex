\sectionbar{Adaptation pipeline and notations}
\footnotesize
For query lookback $X$ and target $T\in\mathbb R^H$, the frozen backbone $F$
produces the vanilla forecast $V=F(X)$. Retrieval returns $K$ historical pairs
$(X_k,Y_k)$ with distance weights $w_k$; their backbone forecasts are
$N_k=F(X_k)$, their weighted future is $\bar Y=\sum_{k=1}^{K}w_kY_k$, and the
retrieved pairs also produce the covariate-conditioned forecast
$C=F(X;\{(X_k,Y_k)\}_{k=1}^{K})$. The adaptor maps
$(V,C,Y_{1:K},N_{1:K},\bar Y)$ to the final forecast $\widehat T$.
For a candidate adaptation $A$, its realized advantage over vanilla is
\[
\Delta(A)=\frac1H\sum_{h=1}^{H}\left[(T_h-V_h)^2-(T_h-A_h)^2\right].
\]

We propose various \textit{baselines} corresponding to different formulas fitted on $C=F(X;\{(X_k,Y_k)\}_{k=1}^{K})$ to predict $T$,
as well as \textit{gates} which take binary decisions to choose between $V$ and a given non-trainable adaptor $A \in \{C, \overline{Y}\}$.

For a given configuration $c$ and method $m$, relative improvement in nMSE
over the vanilla run from the \emph{same retrieval pipeline} is
\[
I_{m,c}=100\frac{\operatorname{nMSE}_{v,c}-\operatorname{nMSE}_{m,c}}
{\operatorname{nMSE}_{v,c}}\ \%.
\]
Tables report the mean of $I_{m,c}$ over the 12 held-out $T_3$ configurations.

\sectionbar{Partial and failed workflows}
\scriptsize
\textbf{TS-RAG (job 43579).} All four released-model inferences completed, but
the table step failed to discover them. Against matching Chronos-Bolt vanilla,
TS-RAG changes nMSE by +0.44\% (Electricity), +0.48\% (Traffic), $-2.01$\%
(Solar), and +8.69\% (Exchange): +1.90\% equally averaged. Against the selected
full-ridge control the mean advantage is only +0.85\%, with losses on Traffic
and Solar. The metric artifacts are valid; the workflow/table is not terminally
successful.

\textbf{Exact-split SOTA benchmark (job 43580).} Five of seven local rows
completed before Electricity retrieval was OOM-killed; Exchange did not start.
The adapted MSE is 0.3446/0.2500/0.3077/0.1494/0.2079 on
ETTh1/ETTh2/ETTm1/ETTm2/Weather. It improves local vanilla on four rows, but its
five-row mean (0.2519) remains above the published TS-RAG/Cross-RAG means
(0.2398/0.2322). This is not yet a complete benchmark.

\textbf{K and horizon sweeps.} Job 43589 stopped on the now-removed $K=100$.
The active $K\leq20$ grid has 60/144 current retrieval extractions complete; a
rerun must finish the remaining 84 before adaptation and tables. Job 43592
completed the raw-$K=1$ gate branch, then collided with the identical
Electricity $504{:}24$ instance-$K=3$ extraction concurrently owned by the L
job. That extraction is now complete and reusable, but H still needs a rerun.
