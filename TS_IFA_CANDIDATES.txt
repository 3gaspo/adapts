# Manual TS-IFA candidate handoff

After inspecting the complete non-meta grid, copy the selected comma-separated
entries into TS_IFA_CANDIDATES_CSV when submitting either ablation or meta front.

Format:
ts_ifa/<retrieval_run>/<joint_method>

Example (replace with the observed winners):
ts_ifa/raw_euclidean_3_online/joint_ridge_shared_softmax_cov,ts_ifa/instance_euclidean_3_online/joint_neural_horizon_unconstrained_full

Allowed method structure:
joint_<ridge|neural>_<shared|horizon>_<unconstrained|softmax>_<branches>

The standard grid uses cov, residual, memory, or full. Direct CLI and selected
runner entries may express another non-empty combination with `+`, such as
cov+residual.
